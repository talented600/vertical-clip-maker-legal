Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vertical Clip Maker by MC ("Vertical Clip Maker", "the software", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the desktop application and its optional social-media publishing features.

1. Local video processing

Vertical Clip Maker is designed primarily as a local desktop application. Video clipping, rendering, local speech transcription, and supported local AI caption/highlight features are intended to run on your computer.

The software does not intentionally upload your source videos to our own servers for AI processing.

2. Social-media account information

If you choose to connect TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or YouTube, the relevant platform may provide the software with authorization tokens, account identifiers, Page or channel information, and permissions needed to perform the actions you approve.

These credentials are used to maintain the connection and to upload or publish content that you explicitly choose to publish.

3. How connected-account credentials are stored

On Windows, Vertical Clip Maker is designed to protect sensitive social-media access tokens and similar credentials using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) before storing them locally in the application's settings.

We do not intentionally publish these credentials in the application's source repository.

4. Information sent to social-media platforms

When you choose to publish a clip, the software may send the selected video, caption, title, description and required technical metadata to the selected platform. That platform then processes the information under its own privacy policy.

5. Information we do not intentionally sell

We do not sell your personal information or social-media account credentials to advertisers or data brokers.

6. Permissions

The software requests social-media permissions only to support features you choose to use, such as identifying the connected account, uploading videos, publishing posts, or managing the authentication session. Availability and exact permission names are controlled by the relevant platform.

7. Revoking access

You can disconnect an account inside Vertical Clip Maker. You can also revoke the application's authorization from the security or connected-app settings of the relevant social-media platform.

8. Data retention

Local application settings remain on your computer until you remove them, disconnect the relevant account, uninstall the application, or otherwise clear the application's stored data. Content uploaded to TikTok, Meta platforms, YouTube or another third-party service is retained according to that service's policies.

9. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce unnecessary exposure of account credentials, including local Windows credential protection. However, no software or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

The software is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through our own services. Users must also comply with the age requirements of the social-media platforms they connect.

11. Third-party privacy policies

Your use of connected services is also subject to the privacy policies of TikTok, Meta (including Facebook and Instagram), Google/YouTube, and any other service you choose to connect.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the software, connected platforms, or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with its latest update date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, you may contact the project through the public legal repository at github.com/talented600/vertical-clip-maker-legal.
